§ 3 · Why this exists

Energy sharing is becoming legally possible, but still operationally manual. The missing layer is matching, pricing, authorization, settlement, and proof.

Signal Implication
Germany, 25 June 2024 Day-ahead power cleared near EUR 492/MWh after market decoupling.
France, same auction Prices near EUR 2.96/MWh exposed how coordination failures create local spreads.
Our layer Local agents automate offer selection, payment, and proof for regulated sharing.

We do not replace wholesale markets. We automate the local remuneration layer.

Implemented system

§ 4 · Built in the hackathon window

Component Role in the transaction
Producer Raspberry Pi reads hardware state, computes solar surplus, prices energy, and releases delivery.
Seller server Hono resource server exposes `/energy/buy` as an x402-protected per-kWh endpoint.
EV agent Autonomously evaluates price and budget, signs the payment, and re-buys in metered chunks.
Mobile app Starts and stops the charging session; shows reasoning, wallet balance, savings, and Lora proof.

The browser client never signs. Money movement stays in the backend agent with policy limits.

Payment rail

§ 5 · Why crypto, Algorand, Regulated Stablecoin and x402

Question Answer
Why not Visa or SEPA They are human checkout and reconciliation rails with per-transaction economics. Paying every small charging chunk is not viable when each payment carries banking or card overhead.
Why crypto Autonomous machines need final settlement, direct transfer, programmable spend limits, and micropayment economics that still work for sub-euro kWh chunks.
Why x402 It makes energy an HTTP-native paid resource: request, receive `402 Payment Required`, attach payment, receive delivery authorization.
Why Algorand Low fees, fast finality, ASA support, GoPlausible facilitator support, and Lora proof make per-kWh settlement practical.
Why regulated stablecoin Energy is priced in cents per kWh. EURD/EURQ gives a euro-denominated unit without card-network fee structure, while staying closer to household bills and accounting.

The hackathon milestone proves one hardware-backed seller and one EV agent can settle real energy payments. The second milestone turns that loop into a local marketplace.

Track Integration path
