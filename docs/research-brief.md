# Research Brief

Owner: J runs the research. B filters findings into product and pitch implications. N checks technical
feasibility. S turns the strongest insights into demo/pitch visuals.

## Research Goal

Find the strongest non-hackathon case for why this project should exist: what similar energy-sharing
or blockchain-energy projects tried, why they struggled, what market/regulatory pain remains, and
why agentic x402 settlement is a credible new angle.

## Output Format

Owner: J. Produce a concise memo with these sections:

1. Prior attempts and lessons.
2. Current unsolved problems in energy sharing and local flexibility.
3. Regulatory constraints and violation risks.
4. Why our design is different.
5. Claims safe enough for pitch/product docs.
6. Claims that need legal or domain verification.

## Legal Framework To Map

| Framework | Owner | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Section 42c EnWG | B | Energy sharing, ct/kWh remuneration, same balancing area, contracts, metering. |
| EEG renewable-only storage rules | B | Battery eligibility when stored electricity is used in sharing. |
| Messstellenbetriebsgesetz | B | 15-minute metering requirement and certified measurement context. |
| EU Renewable Energy Directive / energy communities | B | Broader EU basis for citizen and renewable energy communities. |
| Consumer protection and billing transparency | B | Real users need understandable pricing, invoices, and dispute handling. |
| MiCA / e-money | B | EURQ/stablecoin settlement must be framed carefully in production. |
| GDPR | B | Energy consumption data is sensitive household behavior data. |
| Calibrated metering / EV charging rules | B | Future real charging/payment requires compliant measurement and billing. |
