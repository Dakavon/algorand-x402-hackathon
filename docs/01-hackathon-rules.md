# Hackathon Rules & Guidelines

**Event:** Algorand Builders Berlin — Agentic Commerce x402 Hackathon
**Partner:** 42 Berlin
**Format:** 36-hour event
**Full info:** https://luma.com/agentic-commerce-hack

## Timeline

- **Hacking starts:** 09:00, **6 June 2026**
- **Hacking ends / hard submission deadline:** **13:00 (midday), 7 June 2026**
- All teams that want to qualify for prizes must **stop coding and submit their project +
  presentation materials before 13:00 on 7 June**.

## Rules & requirements

- **Team size:** 1–5 participants.
- **Remote participation:** Allowed, **but at least one team member must be fully on-site and
  present the final pitch.**
- **AI usage:** Fully allowed and encouraged. Algorand AI tooling and templates are provided.
- **Mandatory stack:** All projects **must implement x402 on Algorand** and **adhere to the
  "Agentic Commerce" topic** to qualify for prizes.

## Two-tier project system

- **New Projects:** Start fresh during the hackathon — **no pre-built code**. (Ideation
  beforehand is fine; code must be written during the event.)
- **Existing Projects:** Pre-existing Web2 or Web3 projects that integrate x402 / add Algorand /
  extend the product. Must clearly explain in the submission: what was built before (and over
  what period), what was added during the hackathon, and why.

> **Our team = New Project.** All code written during the event.

## Prize pool — $21,000+ USDC

Nearly 4× larger than last year, with a structure designed to support builders beyond the event.

### 50/50 distribution model

- **50% awarded after the hackathon.**
- **50% unlocked after completing mutually agreed milestones** (defined individually, e.g.
  launching on Algorand **mainnet**).
- If a team chooses not to pursue milestones, unclaimed funds are reallocated to other winning
  teams.

tracks** if aligned.

## Strategic guidance from organizers

- **Prioritise viability, clarity, and quality of implementation** over chasing the maximum
  number of prizes. A focused, well-executed project stands out more than a scattered one
  targeting many tracks.
- The hackathon is intended as a **launching ground** — organizers want to keep supporting
  viable projects afterwards.
- The Algorand Foundation and partners reserve the right to reallocate the prize pool throughout
  the event to best support projects they want in the ecosystem.

## Submission checklist (before 13:00, 7 June)

- [ ] Working code (written during the hackathon) implementing **x402 on Algorand**.
- [ ] Project clearly fits **Agentic Commerce**.
- [ ] Presentation / pitch materials prepared.
- [ ] One team member on-site to present.
- [ ] (If opting into bonus tracks) the relevant integration is demonstrable.
- [ ] Submitted via the official channel before the deadline.
